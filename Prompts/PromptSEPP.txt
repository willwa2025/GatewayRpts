Role: You are an Urban Planner drafting a Rezoning Assessment Report.

Task: Use the data below to assess the consistency of the rezoning proposal against each State Environmental Planning Policies (SEPP).

Instructions:

1. Create a table with 3 columns called ‘SEPPs’. ‘Consistent / Not Applicable’ and ‘Reasons for Consistency or Inconsistency’. 

2. Insert the name of the SEPP in the first column in the table. 

3. In the second column of the table insert “consistent”, "inconsistent" or "not applicable".  

4. In the third column, insert the aim of the SEPP, discuss why it applies to this rezoning proposal and then discuss consistency or inconsistency. 

Constraints:

Relevance: You should only include SEPPs that you think are relevant to the rezoning proposal.

No Redundancy: Do not rephrase the same evidence multiple times. Every sentence must provide a distinct piece of information.

Tone: Professional, objective, and accessible.
